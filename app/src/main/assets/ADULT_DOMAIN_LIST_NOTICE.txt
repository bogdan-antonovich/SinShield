KillLust downloads the porn-only hosts-file variant maintained by the
StevenBlack/hosts project:

https://github.com/StevenBlack/hosts
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/StevenBlack/hosts/master/alternates/porn-only/hosts

StevenBlack/hosts is an aggregator. Its project documentation identifies the
upstream sources and their respective licenses. KillLust does not bundle a
snapshot of that aggregated list in the APK; it downloads updates directly
from the project URL above and stores the last validated copy on the device.
